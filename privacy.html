yt-jellyfin — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-07

yt-jellyfin is a personal tool. Its only users are the owner's own Google accounts.

Data accessed

With the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube, the tool reads the YouTube subscription list (channel IDs and titles) of the owner's own accounts. It removes or adds a subscription only after the owner approves it.

How data is used and stored

Subscription data and OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's home computer. They are used only to keep the owner's own channel list and subscriptions in sync.

Data is not sold or shared, and is not sent to any third party. No analytics or advertising is used.

yt-jellyfin's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local files removes all stored data.
